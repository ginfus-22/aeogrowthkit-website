How we measure and improve

1

Real Questions

We don't make up queries. We use actual questions your buyers ask in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity. The exact phrasing matters.

2

Four Models

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini. Each reads different signals. We test across all four to get the complete picture.

3

Baseline & Gaps

We capture your starting point (how many questions are you cited in?), then identify exactly which gaps are highest-value to fix.

4

Monthly Re-test

Same questions. Same models. Same methodology. Every month so you see trends and prove ROI, not just hope it's working.

5

Competitive Intel

We track who else is being cited in your category. New players. Rising stars. Gaps competitors haven't filled yet.

6

Iterate & Grow

New gaps become new briefs. New content gets tested. The cycle repeats, and your citation rate compounds month after month.

Patterns we see across clients

Technical fixes move fast

Schema fixes, robots.txt unblocking, and crawler access usually show results in 2-4 weeks. The speed surprises people. Most never knew they were blocking AI.

Content format matters more than you think

Your 5,000-word guide won't get quoted by AI. A 1,000-word focused answer will. Short, chunked, answer-first structure beats long-form every time.

Voice beats generic

AI doesn't prefer bland. Clients who write in their actual voice — opinionated, specific, jargony — get cited more often than those who write "for the internet."
