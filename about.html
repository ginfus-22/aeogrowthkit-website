The rules of being found just changed.

For twenty years, getting found meant ranking on Google. Now your buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity, and AI hands back two or three names. Being one of them is the difference between growing and going unseen. And it comes down to something most businesses struggle to keep up with: a steady stream of genuinely good content.

Why AEO Growth Kit exists

The problem

Growing your visibility means publishing content, and doing it properly takes real skill. You need the SEO and structure right, and the substance worth reading. Most busy founders have the time for neither. So they hand it to an LLM, which churns out a vanilla article that ticks a box and reads like everyone else's. Research takes time. Forming a real opinion takes time. Good writing takes time. The volume tools skip all of it, and end up spamming the web with content that quietly damages your brand.

The solution

AEO Growth Kit is the considered alternative: a low-lift, high-quality, sustainable way to make content that earns its place. It combines proven SEO, GEO and AEO principles so every piece lands on both presentation and substance, fills the specific gaps where AI is leaving you out, and keeps your brand visible as AI search takes over. Higher quality, in your voice, in far less of your time.

Who's behind it

AEO Growth Kit is built on methods its founder, Steve Gillson, has spent four years teaching at a Financial Times top-40 business school and applying with real businesses. If a tactic doesn't hold up in the lecture hall and then deliver in practice, it doesn't make it into the product.
