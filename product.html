The Mirror Test runs through everything

Before the three steps, the one thing they share. The AI Mirror Test puts your real buyer questions to four leading AI models and counts how often you're named. Why the order matters

Recover, then Build, then Maintain. The order isn't arbitrary. Build reacts to the gaps Recover finds, so measurement has to come first. Maintain tracks progress against the baseline Recover set, so it can't lead either. Follow the loop and you always know the next move, instead of guessing which lever to pull.
