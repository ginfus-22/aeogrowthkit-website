Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to give site owners information about how their site is used. Similar technologies such as local storage and pixels work in comparable ways, and we refer to all of them as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to keep the site working, to remember your choices, and to understand how visitors use our pages so we can improve them. We only set non-essential cookies (such as analytics) where you have given consent, and you can change your mind at any time.

3. Categories of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

These cookies are required for the site to function — for example to keep you logged in or to remember items during a session. They cannot be switched off in our systems. No consent is required for these.

Functional

These remember choices you make, such as preferences and settings, to give you a better experience.

Analytics

These help us understand how visitors interact with the site by collecting information anonymously, so we can measure and improve performance. They are only set with your consent.

5. Managing your cookies

When you first visit our site, our cookie banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time via the cookie settings link [wire this to your consent banner in Code].

You can also control cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. If you block strictly necessary cookies, some parts of the site may not work properly.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us. For more on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
