Why most AI content never gets mentioned

AI answer engines don't quote content because it's long or stuffed with keywords. They quote content that proves real experience, expertise and authority, the same signals Google calls E-E-A-T. Generic AI-written content fails that test, because there's no real person and no real authority behind it. That's why publishing more of it changes nothing.
